Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Expense Manager ("MobyMoney", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture provided by Google to authenticate and secure your account.
  • Financial & Expense Data: We store your expense entries, amounts, currency selections (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED), categories, and receipt descriptions to provide expense tracking and financial analytics.
  • Receipt Images: If you upload receipts for expense attachment, images are processed securely to extract receipt details.
  • Device Information: General device details (OS version, model) collected for crash reporting and app performance analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, personalize, and improve the expense tracking features of the app.
  • To authenticate users and synchronize transaction records across your devices.
  • To provide offline-first local caching and seamless cloud synchronization.
  • To ensure security and prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and AES-256 encryption via secure storage for local credentials. We do not sell or rent your personal financial data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with trusted third-party providers that adhere to strict data privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase / Google Sign-In: For authentication and secure cloud storage.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You can view, edit, or delete any expense record directly inside the application. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data permanently, you can request account deletion directly from the Profile/Settings screen or by contacting our support.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
